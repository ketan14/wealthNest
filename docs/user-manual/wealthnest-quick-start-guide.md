# WealthNest Quick Start Guide

**Document:** WealthNest 5-Minute Quick Start Guide  
**Audience:** New WealthNest Users & Family Caretakers  
**Reading Time:** 5–7 Minutes  
**Version:** 1.0.0  

---

## Welcome to WealthNest!
WealthNest is a shared family financial workspace. Whether you are managing your personal investments or taking care of your entire family's wealth, this guide walks you through the first four essential steps to establish your family financial command center.

---

## Step 1: Create Your Account & Understand Your Household

1. Navigate to WealthNest in your browser or launch the desktop application.
2. Click **Create an account** or visit `/signup`.
3. Enter your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and choose a secure password (minimum 12 characters with uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols).
4. Click **Create Secure Account**.
5. Once signed in, you are automatically assigned your personal **Household Workspace** as the **Primary Owner**.

> **Important Concept:** A **Household** is your family's private financial space. You can manage everything yourself, or invite other family members to join with their own login accounts whenever you choose.

---

## Step 2: Set Up Your Family Member Profiles

Before adding investments or property, set up the people or entities who legally own them:

1. Click on **Settings** in the left sidebar (or tap the **Settings** gear on mobile).
2. Select the **Family & Demats** tab.
3. Click the **Manage Family Profiles** button.
4. For each family member (such as yourself, your spouse, a minor child, an elderly parent, or a family HUF):
   - Click **Add Family Member**.
   - Enter their **Full Legal Name** and select their **Relationship** (Self, Spouse, Child, Parent, Sibling, Other).
   - Select their **Entity Type** (`Individual`, `Minor`, or `HUF`).
   - *(Optional)* Enter their PAN number for tax tracking and portfolio identification.
   - Click **Add Member**.

> **Note:** Family members do **not** need their own email addresses or login accounts to have a profile. You can record assets in their name immediately!

---

## Step 3: Add Your First Assets & Investments

WealthNest allows you to track mutual funds, equities, fixed deposits, gold, and properties:

### Option A: Manual Entry
1. In the sidebar, click **Portfolio**.
2. Click the green **+ Add Holding** button in the top right.
3. Select the **Asset Class** (e.g., *Mutual Funds*, *Indian Equities*, *Fixed Deposits*, or *Gold*).
4. Enter the **Asset Name** (e.g., "HDFC Top 100 Index Fund" or "Reliance Industries").
5. Enter your **Quantity / Units**, **Average Purchase Cost (₹)**, and **Current Market Value (₹)**.
6. **Assign Ownership:** Choose who legally owns this holding from your family list. If co-owned, use **Add Co-Owner** to split ownership percentages (e.g. 50% Self, 50% Spouse).
7. **Set Privacy:** Select `Household Shared` (visible to all family members) or `Owner Only` (private to you).
8. Click **Save Holding**.

### Option B: Fast Import via CAS Statement (Mutual Funds)
If you invest in mutual funds, you can import your entire portfolio in 60 seconds:
1. On the **Portfolio** page, click the **Import CAS** button.
2. Select your Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) PDF downloaded from CAMS or KFintech.
3. Enter your PDF password (typically your PAN in uppercase or email address).
4. Review the preview of detected mutual fund folios.
5. Click **Confirm & Import** to automatically create your holdings!

---

## Step 4: Add Properties, Loans & Inspect Your Net Worth

1. **Add Real Estate:** Click **Real Estate** in the sidebar, click **+ Add Property**, and enter your home's purchase price and estimated market value.
2. **Add Home Loan / Liabilities:** Click **Loans & Liabilities**, click **+ Add Liability**, select *Home Loan*, enter your current outstanding principal, interest rate, and monthly EMI. You can link this loan directly to your property to track **Property Equity**!
3. **Inspect Your Dashboard:** Click **System Overview** (Dashboard) in the sidebar. You will see:
   - **Total Net Worth:** `Total Assets` minus `Total Liabilities`.
   - **Asset Allocation:** Clean visual breakdown of Equities, Mutual Funds, Real Estate, and Cash.
   - **Privacy Mode:** Click the **Eye icon** in the top header at any time to mask all rupee figures when viewing your screen in public!
